## Video direction

Quiet, sensual beauty-editorial on off-white stone (#ecebe5). Product photos sit on the canvas with
`mix-blend-mode: multiply` so their white studio sweep dissolves into the page. Jelly red (#a3142c) is
the one loud colour — used for one hero word per frame and the full-bleed taste frame. Assistant
(Hebrew, RTL), light 300 for display, 600 for labels. Motion is slow and soft: power3/expo eases,
gentle push-ins under every shot, per-word rises with slight blur. No narration; each frame reveals on
the beat. No price claims, no results claims.
